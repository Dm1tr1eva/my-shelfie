What we store

To run your catalogue, my-shelfie keeps:

  • your account: name and email address, and a password hash if you sign up with email;
  • your books and series: titles, authors, covers, series numbers, ownership, reading status, ratings, genres and tropes;
  • what you write: notes, annotations and shop links.

Your shelves are private. Other users can’t see them.

Google sign-in and search

If you choose Continue with Google, we receive your name and email from Google and nothing else. [CONFIRM SCOPES]

When you add a book, your search text is sent to Google Books to find matches. The result you pick is saved to your shelf.

Shop links

Links are stored exactly as you paste them. my-shelfie doesn’t track clicks and doesn’t use affiliate links. [CONFIRM]
